## 3. Platform-Adapted Versions

### Instagram Reels
- Same base script. Add a 3-second aesthetic beat before the hook: quick shot of the serum bottle catching morning light, then cut to selfie for the hook line.
- Suggested audio vibe: soft, warm acoustic or lo-fi — "getting ready in the morning" mood, nothing high-energy.
- Text overlay timing: hook text appears at 0:00–0:03, proof-point overlay ("10% stabilized Vitamin C") at 0:18–0:22, CTA text at 0:32 onward.
- *Rationale: Reels audiences tolerate a beat of polish before the talking starts — full raw TikTok pacing slightly underperforms here.*

### TikTok
- Cut the aesthetic beat entirely — hook must be the very first frame, spoken within 1–2 seconds of the video starting.
- Direction note: handheld, slightly imperfect angle, ordinary bathroom/bedroom background is fine — do not shoot this like a Reel.
- Text overlays as bold, short caption bursts (3–5 words at a time) rather than full sentences, appearing under the chin.
- *Rationale: TikTok's algorithm and audience punish anything that reads as "produced" in the first second.*

### YouTube Shorts
- Same base script, but extend the "problem" section by 3–4 seconds with one more relatable detail (e.g., "especially in pictures with flash") — Shorts viewers tolerate more setup before payoff.
- Add a verbal CTA in addition to text: "It's linked below if you want to look into it" — Shorts drives more description-link clicks than swipe-through purchases.
- Runtime can extend to ~48 seconds.
- *Rationale: Shorts audiences arrive via search/suggested more than pure scroll, so slightly more explanation earns rather than loses attention.*

## 5. A/B Variation Set

### Variant A — Results-Led
**New hook:** "Day 1 versus day 14. I wasn't expecting this."
**What changed:** Opens directly on the before/after comparison instead of the personal confession; problem section shortened to one line; moves proof point ("2 weeks") to the first 10 seconds instead of the middle.
**Best tested against:** Viewers who've already tried multiple products and are numb to claims — they want visual/timeframe proof immediately.
**Hypothesis:** Likely higher watch-through in the first 5 seconds, but may get fewer saves/shares since it's less relatable/story-driven.

### Variant B — Price/Value-Led
**New hook:** "I stopped buying the ₹2,000 serum for this reason."
**What changed:** Adds a value-comparison line in the solution section ("costs less than what I was buying before, and I'm actually finishing the bottle this time"); CTA shifts slightly toward "worth trying before you rebuy the expensive one."
**Best tested against:** Price-conscious segment — lookalike audiences built from past discount/sale-code engagement.
**Hypothesis:** Could outperform on click-through for price-sensitive audiences but may attract lower-intent, discount-only buyers.

### Variant C — Emotion/Identity-Led
**New hook:** "Why does everyone's skin look brighter than mine in photos?"
**What changed:** Reframes the problem section around how dull skin *feels* in social situations (photos, comparison, confidence) rather than describing the skin functionally; solution section adds one line about "not thinking about it in photos anymore."
**Best tested against:** Younger segment (22–27) more active on Reels/TikTok, more responsive to social-comparison framing.
**Hypothesis:** Likely to perform best on shares/comments (relatable pain point) but should be monitored for sounding too insecurity-driven — soften if comments skew negative.
